# Privacy and Data Provenance

## Synthetic data only

All names, passport numbers, dates, customer identifiers, OCR outputs, and processing outcomes in this repository are synthetic and generated specifically for demonstration. They do not describe real people or real travel documents.

## Excluded materials

This repository must not contain:

- Client-provided files, even when described as test or virtual documents
- Real or customer-assigned identity data
- Company or client source code, document templates, logos, credentials, endpoints, or internal configuration
- Proprietary business rules, thresholds, operational volumes, or performance metrics
- Images designed to imitate a valid government identity document

## Independent implementation

The demo is independently implemented for educational and portfolio purposes. Its workflow is simplified and based on general concepts such as OCR output normalization, record matching, conflict detection, and human-in-the-loop review.

## No production claims

Results calculated from synthetic data are illustrative. They must not be presented as evidence of production OCR accuracy, customer outcomes, operational savings, or deployment performance.
